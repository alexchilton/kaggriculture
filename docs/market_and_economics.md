# Market and economics

Settled facts on the price/sell mechanism, the 2026-08-15 hinge balance change, crop-mix
economics (carrot/tomato/egg/melon), simulator parity, pen geometry, and shed/wheat-churn defects.
Full detail for every line below is in `docs/history/`, named per line.

## Settled

- `market_price(item, inventory, params)` is a pure function of inventory, monotone decreasing above `I0 = 10,000` (`reference/kaggriculture.py:177-191`); sales at the $1 floor do not raise market inventory — the floor is an infinite sink. — `history/the_market_2026-08-09.md`
- `FEED` consumes exactly 1 WHEAT per animal per day from the shed (`kaggriculture.py:505-512`), so wheat is a consumable as well as a market product. — `history/market_curves_and_the_q4_branch_2026-08-15.md`
- `_process_market` drains ONE shared market object per unit in lockstep between both seats (`kaggriculture.py:544-547`); every unit either seat sells lowers the other's price. — `history/market_curves_and_the_q4_branch_2026-08-15.md`
