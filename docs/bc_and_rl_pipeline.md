# BC/imitation pipeline and the RL framing

Settled facts on the problem classification, measured env/sim throughput, the parked offline
chain-search stack, the 2026-08-20 BC pipeline defects, the imitation/DAgger plan, and the two
same-day bundle-head reviews. The RL kill-criteria ruling itself is NOT here — see
`docs/rl_feasibility.md` and `docs/kill_criteria_and_settled_positions.md`.
Full detail for every line below is in `docs/history/`, named per line.

## Settled

- Kaggriculture is classified as a deterministic, open-loop, multi-dimensional knapsack with a time-phased budget played as a best response to a fixed field, not an RL problem: the leaderboard top submits a fixed 720-turn tape (four fresh leader replays are byte-identical in every derived quantity across four opponents and both seats), the env is deterministic given the tape, and the reward is terminal/Elo-shaped (a $43k win and a $4k win score identically). — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- Greedy-by-payback under a cash simulation recovers only 23% of what a beam search finds — the schedule genuinely needs search even though the allocation is near-additive and computable in closed form. — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- Cash timing is not a mild coupling between chains: measured, it is worth roughly 4x the allocation itself — additivity buys the crop/animal mix, it does not buy the day-by-day schedule. — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- Six same-day misdiagnoses were all limitations of the offline chain builder, not facts about the game: "animals don't pay" (fertilizer never collected), "fertilizer doesn't help" (FERTILIZE never ordered), "day 0 is right for land" (hardcoded catalog index), "strawberry loses to melon" (a watering-frequency bug), "four pens is right" (slot-accounting bug), "weeds cost nothing" (true only on farms that never replant). — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- Reference-env (Python) throughput measured 2026-07-31: the training box (Ryzen 9 5900HX) does 484 steps/s single-threaded, 4,221 with 16 workers (8.7x scaling on 8c/16t) — 2.2x slower per core and 1.9x slower in aggregate than the dev Mac's 1,054/8,009; `README.md`'s recorded 1,163 steps/s was the Mac rate, 2.4x optimistic for the training box. To reach 10B steps in 24 hours needs ~116,000 steps/s, ~27x the Python env's best multiprocess rate — the vectorized sim is required, not optional. — `history/throughput_measurements.md`
- CORRECTED: the original "1,032x" numpy-sim speedup figure was an idle-profile artifact — measured with one live unit per player on an empty board, hitting an `if not active.any(): continue` shortcut almost every update. Re-measured with units actually planting: 818,000 steps/s at 1 live unit, dropping to 356,000 at 24; an independent run with units spread out and hiring every turn (more realistic) measured ~15,000 steps/s. Trust the ratio (30x-200x over the reference env), not the absolute headline number. — `history/throughput_measurements.md`
- Offline schedule-search stage 1 (`chains.py`/`scheduler.py`) was reviewed and found structurally broken, not merely mistuned: its best saved genome banks $2,484 and wins 0/16 because `decode()` emits `buy_land` orders unconditionally while `reachable_quadrants` gates chains on a DIFFERENT, uncrewed cash model that reads ~$108 on day 11 against the real ~$1,040 needed — the plan pays for parcels it then refuses every chain that would use. — `history/fable_stage1_review_2026-08-09.md`
- PARKED, not fixed: stage 1 optimizes a proxy (`revenue_rate`) inside a proxy (a decode step holding an independent veto) against a phantom opponent pool (predates the real cluster data, contains none of the three real archetypes) — it has never shipped and its best output loses 16/16 to its own sibling agent; keep the chains/decode/sim machinery as an asset, retire the cash-model scoring method. — `history/fable_stage1_review_2026-08-09.md`
- BC pipeline defects found 2026-08-20 (nine total, seven invisible to a passing test suite): `fit_bc` was missing `has_aux=True` and could not have taken a single gradient step; every SELL was emitted LAST at play time because a sorted bundle puts alphabetical BUY_* before SELL while the env settles order slots in sequence, so a student replaying the teacher's exact bundle had its hires refused on a thin bank; `build_bc_rows` was OOM-killed silently on all 16 workers by loading a 1.2GB shard into a list of dicts before sizing arrays. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- Market quantities were rounded to unmeasured ladder rungs (`SELL_BUCKETS=[0,1,3,10,30]`) with 27.2% mean projection error on SELL, 35.3% on BUY_PRODUCT — the true distribution is flat (SELL uses every value 1-24 with no gaps), so a categorical head with class index = quantity (capped at 64) is exact for 99.79% of SELL and 97.50% of BUY_PRODUCT. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- The play-time legality mask vetoed sells the teacher makes constantly: 48.75% of the teacher's own SELL orders have a pre-turn shed of zero for that product (agents re-offer an unfilled quota every hour), and zero of those coincide with a same-turn DROP — training was permissive (teacher's action legal by construction) while the mask was strict, so the masked argmax at play time silently substitutes a DIFFERENT, never-taught trade 52% of sell-turns. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- The "0.788 bundle agreement" number means nothing on its own: an UNTRAINED, zero-initialized network also scores 0.788, because every head defaults to argmax index 0 (the empty bundle), which the teacher plays on 78.9% of turns — without a fresh-init baseline on the same rows, "learned the prior" and "learned nothing" are the identical number. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- 13 of 115 input features are identically zero across 20,000 measured rows (TOMATO tile/item/seed/shed, GOOSE tile/item/shed, EGG item/shed) because the teacher never demonstrates tomato or geese — imitation cannot reach a mechanic the teacher never plays, at any data volume; the Kaggle replay corpus does contain both (164 PLANT TOMATO turns in 17/25 games, 45 BUY_ANIMAL GOOSE in 7/25) but at 0.02% of a naive mixture with the planner shards, needing upweighting, not concatenation. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- DAgger labelling is cheap in principle: forcing every day through the live solver costs only 1.2x (0.0108 vs 0.0092 s/turn), not the ~100x originally assumed — 640 games on 16 workers solved in ~5 minutes against ~4 shipped. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- The tape-clone track is closed and superseded by teacher-based imitation: a `(day,hour,unit)` lookup table reproduces kawashigi exactly to d12 then rots (crops 53->1, weeds 0->48 by d29) because it is keyed on the open-loop sequence, not the board — using the shipped ~$90k-class agent as a QUERYABLE teacher retires the earlier objection that no teacher ever emits FEED usefully. — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- The teacher smoke-tested clean: 720 steps both seats DONE, banks $142,002/$147,440, 29.4s per mirror season (0.04s/turn) — but this cost figure DEPENDS on `UNSOLVE_DAYS = range(24,30)`, meaning only d24-d29 are solved live and d0-d23 replay `PRECOMPUTED_ROUTES`; querying the teacher on a student's divergent pre-d24 state returns route slots for a board the student does not have, and `cover._apply_precomputed` drops mismatches SILENTLY — DAgger's corrective labels would be wrong exactly where correction is the point, at ~2 orders of magnitude more labelling cost if fixed (full live solving). — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`, `history/fable_bundle_head_review_2026-08-20.md`
- The teacher is sound in the late game on its OWN trajectory, addressing the tape-rot concern directly: our planner holds 57.5 crops / 2.4 weeds at d18 (kawashigi holdout: 51 crops / 1 weed; the dead tape-clone: 18 crops / 34 weeds) and 16.0 crops / 3.6 weeds at d29 — it never weeds over; the deficit against kawashigi is in the economy (banks $87,149 mean in uncontested mirror self-play, measured elsewhere as ~2x inflated), not routing or watering. — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- Market-as-one-token, sized on the planner teacher itself (5,752 turns): 21.3% non-empty, only 117 distinct bundles (vs tetsuya's 346 in the field data), top 100 cover 99.70% — a 128-class bundle head replaces six independent heads whose joint decode was measured to emit nothing; the costliest bundle to get wrong is rank 2, `HIRE x10` as ONE symbol (the exact d10 failure where units 8/9 never hired). — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- Bundle-head branch review (2026-08-20): `masks_for` (the legality mask) has NEVER executed — it reads `state.private.shed` on a `State` with no `private` field at all (real field is `state.farm.shed`, width `N_ITEMS=12` not `N_PRODUCTS=9`) — the one existing test never calls it, another instance of "a gate only tests what it exercises." — `history/fable_bundle_head_review_2026-08-20.md`
- SUPERSEDED note carried into the sibling review: because `masks_for` never executed, the sibling review's P1-1 section (measuring the sell-mask's 48.75%/52.13% collision rate) analyzes a mechanism that could not have run in the branch under test at the time — its P0 order-sort bug and most P2 findings are independent of the crash and still stand. — `history/fable_bundle_head_review_2026-08-20.md`, `history/fable_bc_pipeline_review_2026-08-20.md`
- P0, confirmed independent of the mask crash and still live: `orders_from_heads` emits bundle orders in alphabetically-sorted order (BUY_ANIMAL < BUY_LAND < BUY_PRODUCT < BUY_SEED < HIRE < SELL) while the real env settles order slots in sequence and both simulators canonically sell FIRST — so a student replaying the teacher's exact bundle attempts buys/hires before that turn's sell has funded them; invisible in sim-side evaluation because the sim decoders are correct, visible only against the real `kaggle_environments` env. — `history/fable_bc_pipeline_review_2026-08-20.md`
- `bc_dagger.py`'s in-scan loss is missing both padding corrections `bc.py` documents and applies correctly: unit heads are weighted by `unit_live * legal` only (no `active_unit_heads` factor, training the crop head toward WHEAT on non-PLANT slots), and quantity heads score on all products every turn with label 0 on ~79% empty turns, which the floor-at-1 decode turns into "every sell/buy is quantity 1." The 920k-row run goes through `fit_bc.py`, which has both weightings right, so this does not corrupt the imminent run — but any future DAgger round labelled via `bc_dagger.py` would be wasted. — `history/fable_bc_pipeline_review_2026-08-20.md`
- The bundle vocabulary (197 bundles) is not pinned to any checkpoint: the artifact file was rewritten the same day (16:49) it was measured, `fit_bc.py`'s checkpoint stores only `vocab_size`, and `neural.make_agent` loads whatever the file currently contains with no comparison — a checkpoint trained against an earlier vocab build would silently reinterpret every bundle index at play. — `history/fable_bc_pipeline_review_2026-08-20.md`
- The `fit_bc` training-health instrument can be satisfied by a model that fires on the wrong turns entirely: it probes only training data (no held-out shard) and compares marginal empty-rate only, so a model firing non-empty 21% of the time on the WRONG 21% of turns matches the teacher's 0.789 exactly, and it reads none of the quantity heads at all. — `history/fable_bc_pipeline_review_2026-08-20.md`
- Order of proposed work, stated in the plan: (1) the arm classifier / bundle-head fixes are prerequisite — nothing else is interpretable until the branch actually executes; (2) perturbation scoring in `jax_sim`; (3) RL from the tape as a KL-anchored prior, terminal reward, gated on win rate against a frozen pool — doing RL before the imitation layer works means hill-climbing a known-broken copy and confounding every result with its defects. — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`

- The environment's structural constraint table (as classified): 100 tiles non-overlapping (25 owned at spawn, 75 after two land parcels), one action chain per unit slot, 23 work turns/unit-day, $3,000 starting cash as a FLOW not a stock, land at $1,000/$2,000/$4,000 in fixed order, hires priced by a daily-resetting Fibonacci curve (12 hands is $376/day, 17 is $4,180/day). — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- The tool ordering as originally designed for the offline stack: compute the allocation target (`allocation_calculator.py`), beam-search chains for the exact joint max per slot (`beam_chains.py`, weak at restructuring), CEM/ES to restructure out of that basin (`search_chains.py --seed-genome`), then verify on held-out seeds in the real environment (`arena_gate.py`) — not optional. — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- `search_chains.py` had an overfitting defect until 2026-08-05: the board draw was cached on one seed, so 30 generations of 500 candidates all fit the same 16 games and the incumbent could never be displaced; fixed by drawing a new board every generation and adding a `--holdout` pass that prints the in-search vs held-out gap. — `history/what_kind_of_problem_this_is.md`
- Stage-1 review found four more independent defects beyond the headline decode veto: `to_genome` silently truncates (a v7 run scheduled 14 chains into 12 slots and the last two vanished uncounted); the per-chain drain cap is not shared across chains of the same product (four melon chains "sell" 4x15 units into a single 30-unit drain); `OPPONENT_SHARE=0.5` vs a `--share 1.0` flag disagree by 2x about market size between the mix step and the cash-model step; and CARE does not exist in the revenue model at all, so a build like HealthStone's (rank #2, built on CARE compounding from sheep) is invisible to it. — `history/fable_stage1_review_2026-08-09.md`
- Highest-EV sequence recommended by the stage-1 review, given the cluster-analysis floor is a public agent (`kaitofukami v23`, replicable locally at 3,117-3,131): put v23 in the arena as an opponent and measure win rate first, then hand-build the opening from cluster signatures to reach that band (~2,000 rating points before any originality), then search for a counter — since Seb ranked #1 while banking the LEAST money by consistently beating the v23 cluster, which is exactly the kind of edge an exact simulator plus search should find. — `history/fable_stage1_review_2026-08-09.md`
- The parked BC track's signature failure mode is a single number: `P(move) = 6e-5 per unit-turn`, meaning across 13 units and 30 days you would expect roughly ONE unit to ever leave the shed — a fast, cheap first check (argmax distribution on one batch) that catches a dead label alignment or loss before a long training run. — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- Normalisation on the real BC feature set is sound (20,000 rows checked): tile features range [-0.900, 1.750] std 0.269, unit features [-1.000, 2.699] std 0.259, global features [-1.394, 4.500] std 0.442, zero features exceed |max|>10, zero inf/nan, nothing within 10% of the float16 ceiling — the agent also plays 2,876 real-env turns with zero exceptions, zero out-of-slot-order orders, zero bad quantities. — `history/the_bc_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- `bc.py` already carries three hard-won loss fixes worth preserving as a checklist for any future rewrite: illegal labels hitting `NEG_INF=-1e9` (would produce a 5.3e7 loss that cannot move), padding slots making ~85% of the objective constant, and conditioned heads scored under every trade mode rather than only mode 0 — but there is NO KL anchor in `bc_dagger.py`, which matters for stage 3 where PPO can walk off the imitation prior in the first few updates. — `history/the_imitation_pipeline_2026-08-20.md`
- The bundle-head review found `kagg/agents/neural.py` (the real-environment play path) was never migrated to the new bundle-head design: its local `MARKET_HEADS` constant is still the old six-head layout and `decode_action` reads keys (`heads["sell"]`, `heads["hire"]`, `heads["buy_land"]`) the new `Policy` no longer emits — this blocks any out-of-scan DAgger, submission, or replay check until fixed, a prerequisite, not cleanup. — `history/fable_bundle_head_review_2026-08-20.md`
- Seven-plus call sites in the bundle-head branch still use the old one-argument `masks_for` signature, including `train_jax_rl.py` (the only leg that can beat the teacher) and `play_checkpoint.py` (the plan's own main dead-policy detector) — red tests would have caught this but the suite was not run after the signature change. — `history/fable_bundle_head_review_2026-08-20.md`
- The quantity-bucket ladders in the old six-head design could not express the teacher at all: `BUY_BUCKETS=[0,1,2,5]` maps a real order of `BUY_SEED CARROT 8 / WHEAT 9` to bucket 5 for both — a ~40% under-buy on the exact purchase that gates planting for the day. — `history/fable_bundle_head_review_2026-08-20.md`
- What came back clean in the bundle-head pipeline review: the quantity round-trip (clamp to 63, never wraps, all three decoders floor at 1), HIRE handled consistently everywhere as a count not a flag, the live 197-bundle vocabulary (no bundle exceeds the 10-order cap, no repeated non-HIRE pair, zero pattern-aliased groups), and `bc.loss`'s weighting (correct as specified, the asymmetry with play-time masking is the real problem). — `history/fable_bc_pipeline_review_2026-08-20.md`
