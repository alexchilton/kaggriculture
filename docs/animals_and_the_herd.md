# Animals and the herd

Settled facts on the herd (cow/sheep/goose) mix question, the geese verdict, and pen-placement
geometry. Read `docs/geese_verdict_status_2026-08-22.md` for the current, live status of the geese
question — it postdates every file merged here and is not touched by this pass.
The geese question has been re-derived at least six times in this project; two more derivations
live in `docs/market_and_economics.md` (`the_market_is_the_late_season_2026-08-13.md` and
`the_mix_and_the_routes_are_one_artifact_2026-08-19.md`).
Full detail for every line below is in `docs/history/`, named per line.

- Suppression-value modelling over 4,000 draws: goose margin expectation is $11,758 WORSE than cow/sheep per six animals, because our marginal milk/wool units force pro-rata floor-sharing onto the opponent's LARGER flow (1.7x our volume) while eggs are uncontested and safe-and-harmless — "safe-and-harmless is exactly what a zero-sum ladder does not pay for." Goose is margin-best in only 0.9% of 4,000 draws (COW 43.6%, SHEEP 55.5%), rising to only 7.0% at its single best observable corner (no YARN_STORE, no milk carrier among the first three shops by d9, 12.8% of games) — no observable rescues it. — `history/fable_geese_audit_2026-08-15.md`
