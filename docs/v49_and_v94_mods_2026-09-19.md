The two effects oppose each other. +173 at t=8.11 is the residual.

## 4. V49's two new layers do not port onto v94

V49 is the V48 chassis (`_e335_agent`) plus two layers.

`_e346_funded` releases a protected future sale once current cash covers a conservative quote for every purchase in the look window plus a $1,000 reserve.
It reads the forward tape through `_adv_future`.
**v94 has no `_adv_future`.**
v94 already carries `_r97_budget`, which is the same conservative-quote budget for a single turn, so `_e346` is the multi-turn extension of something v94 has in single-turn form, and porting it means porting the tape reader too.

`_e342_weed` monkeypatches `Chassis._weed_repair`, and every dependency it needs is present in v94 (`_tile_at`, `st['pending']`, `self.routes`, `view.positions`).
It is still the wrong port.
v94's own `_weed_repair` (line 515) DIGs immediately, queues the intended action in `st["pending"]`, replays it when the unit still stands there and its tape action is a no-op, preserves PLANT to WATER chains, and converts a wasted weed turn into a DIG.
Ahmed's version replays the unit's *previous* command until the tape yields a PASS, and calls `st['pending'].pop(k, None)` twice.
That pop destroys the queue v94's repair uses to remember the intended PLANT.
Read from the source, not measured.
