# `sh:wheat` first diverges at day 8: it is a shed-delivery routing artifact, not a harvest or market shortfall

## Task

`sh:wheat` (wheat in the shed) is exact against Seb's tape for days 0-7 and first diverges at
day 8 (ours ends the day 1 unit lower: 9 vs 10).
Diagnose why, per the reading order the task specified: the shed only gains product via
`_drop_inventories_to_shed` at end-of-day (`reference/kaggriculture.py:812`) and via a filled
`BUY_PRODUCT`, and only loses it via a filled `SELL` (`~line 630`, inside `_process_market`).
`HARVEST` puts units in the acting unit's carried inventory (`private["inventories"][idx]`), not
the shed.
So `sh:wheat` differing can mean three different things: less was harvested, the same was
harvested but it was still in a hand at dusk, or more was sold / less was bought.

## Which of harvest / stranded-in-hand / market explains day 8

`breakdown.py`'s `sell`/`buy_product` rows are **fill lower bounds** (dirty turns dropped, see
the fills doc) -- not reliable enough to settle a 1-unit question.
Wrote `scripts/wheat_d8_probe3.py`, which reads the real quantities directly off
`reference/kaggriculture.py`'s only four channels that can move `private["shed"]["WHEAT"]`:
explicit `DROP`, the dusk auto-drop (`_drop_inventories_to_shed`), `PICKUP`, and the net market
delta from `_process_market` (`SELL` down, `BUY_PRODUCT`/`BUY_ANIMAL` up -- `BUY_ANIMAL` never
touches `WHEAT` so this is effectively `SELL`+`BUY_PRODUCT`).
It wraps each of those four functions, diffs `shed["WHEAT"]` before/after **only for the object
identified as our seat's private dict**, and sums per day.

Getting to a trustworthy read required two false starts, both left in the script's comments so
they aren't rediscovered:

- `private is OUR_PRIVATE[0]` captured once after `env.reset()` goes stale within one step --
  `kaggle_environments` reconstructs the observation object every turn, confirmed by an identity
  probe (`id()` differs before/after every single `env.step()`). Fixed by refreshing the pointer
  at the top of every `interpreter` call instead.
- Monkeypatching `kg.interpreter` (the module attribute) has **zero effect** -- `make()` reads the
  interpreter out of `kaggle_environments.core.environments["kaggriculture"]["interpreter"]`, a
  registry dict populated once at package import with a direct reference to the *original*
  function object. A 0-calls-recorded probe caught this before it produced a wrong answer.
  Fixed by mutating `kaggle_environments.core.environments["kaggriculture"]["interpreter"]`
  directly.

With both fixed, the accounting identity `start + DROP + dusk_drop + PICKUP + PLACE + market ==
end` holds **exactly** for every one of the 12 days, both sides (`chk == OK` throughout,
`data/logs/wheat_d8_probe3.log`) -- meaning these four channels are provably the complete list of
what can move shed WHEAT, with no unaccounted term.

Day 8 row (the first divergent day):

| side | start | DROP | dusk_drop | PICKUP | PLACE | market | end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ours | 9 | +2 | 0 | -12 | 0 | +10 | **9** |
| tape | 9 | +1 | +3 | -13 | 0 | +10 | **10** |

- `start` is identical (9 vs 9) -- confirms d7 really was exact, not coincidentally rounding to
  the same printed value.
- `market` (net `SELL`+`BUY_PRODUCT` WHEAT) is **identical**: +10 both sides. Rules out "sold
  more / bought less" for day 8 specifically.
- `HARVEST` op count is identical (2 vs 2, from `scripts/wheat_d8_probe1.py`,
  `data/logs/wheat_d8_probe1.log`). Rules out "harvested less."
- The gap is entirely inside `DROP + dusk_drop + PICKUP`: ours nets `2 + 0 - 12 = -10` into the
  shed via those three channels, tape nets `1 + 3 - 13 = -9`. `-10 - (-9) = -1`, exactly the
  observed gap, with no residual.

## Stated cause

**Day 8's `sh:wheat` gap is a shed-delivery routing/timing artifact, not a production or market
shortfall: both sides harvest the same amount and net-trade the same amount of wheat that day
(`HARVEST` 2/2, market fill +10/+10), but our live router explicitly `DROP`s wheat mid-day and
ends the day with nothing left to sweep (`dusk_drop=0`), while the tape's recorded actions leave
3 units uncollected until the dusk auto-drop (`dusk_drop=+3`) and additionally `PICKUP` 1 more
unit than ours -- and that timing/pickup mix, not harvest or trade, nets to exactly the observed
-1.**

This is not "wheat stranded in our hands" (the task's literal hypothesis 2) -- it is the *tape's*
crew that ends day 8 holding wheat until dusk, not ours; ours has already delivered it earlier the
same day via an explicit `DROP`. The two systems (a live router reading board state each turn vs.
a fixed recorded tape) simply sequence an equivalent day's work differently, and the difference in
sequencing happens to net to a non-zero shed count for the first time on day 8, after 8 days where
`pickup`/`drop`/`pass`/`move` op counts already differed but canceled out to zero effect on
`sh:wheat`.

## Why no fix was attempted

The `DROP`/`dusk_drop`/`PICKUP` split is not a single identifiable bug -- both sides' numbers are
individually plausible crew activity (2 explicit drops, some pickups for a same-day carry), and
there is no crisp `assert X` theory of "our code does Y which is wrong" to write a failing pytest
against, which the project's testing discipline requires before any code change. Forcing our live
router to reproduce the tape's exact turn-by-turn `DROP`/`PICKUP` sequence would mean cloning the
tape's routing decisions, not fixing a defect -- well outside what this task asked for. The task's
bounds explicitly permit stopping after a stated, measured cause with **at most** one fix attempt,
not exactly one; given no crisp bug, none was made. No code was changed; `d0-d7` cannot have
regressed because nothing was touched.

## What was NOT checked

- **Days 9-11**, where `sh:wheat` keeps diverging (-5, -5, -8). Spot-checked with the same probe
  (rows are in `data/logs/wheat_d8_probe3.log`) and the *dominant* term is different at d9: market
  nets +6 for us vs +10 for the tape (a 4-unit gap), not the `DROP`/`PICKUP`/`dusk_drop` triad
  that explained d8. Per the project's selection rule, a later day regressing for its own reason
  is a separate problem with its own origin, not a veto on the d8 diagnosis, and is explicitly out
  of scope here.
- **Why** the live router chooses to `DROP` mid-day on d8 while the tape doesn't, or why it picks
  up one fewer unit -- i.e., the specific line(s) in `kagg/search/router.py` /
  `kagg/search/schedule_agent.py` that produce that choice. Only the WHEAT-quantity consequence
  was measured, not the routing decision's source.
- Whether the extra unit the tape picks up on d8 gets `FEED`-consumed downstream (which would make
  its non-return to the shed intentional rather than "stranded"). Not traced past the shed
  boundary.
- Any other crop/product's day-8 behavior -- this was scoped to WHEAT only, per the task.
- Whether the day-0-7 op-count differences (`pickup`, `drop`, `pass`, `move`) that already exist
  but net to zero on `sh:wheat` originate from a single common cause (e.g. accumulated RNG-stream
  drift) or several independent ones. Not investigated.
