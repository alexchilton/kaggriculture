### 2. Twelve sheep is more wool than the town can drain

The same latch also selects the herd through `WOOL_SHOP_SETS_THE_HERD`: cows capped at
`WOOL_SHOP_COW_CAP`, everything else to sheep.
We ran **6 COW / 12 SHEEP**; Koba ran **13 COW / 9 SHEEP**.

Per head, the cows won:

              animals   units   revenue   per head
    COW             6     141   $14,476     $2,413
    SHEEP          12     230   $24,719     $2,060

And that is despite WOOL's base being **$200** against MILK's **$160**.
We realised **$107** for wool (54% of base) and **$103** for milk (64%).

The price series says why:

    day  WOOL px  MILK px  WOOL inv  MILK inv    (I0 = 10,000)
     14      239      185      9,910     9,992
     20      227      177      9,979     9,996
     22      195      143     10,009    10,008   <- wool crosses I0
     24      148      172     10,030     9,998
     26       24      169     10,055     9,999
     28       11      181     10,057     9,994

**$239 to $11, a 95% collapse, on an overshoot of 57 units above I0.**
WOOL is `above_func: sq, above_target: 3.20, T: 105` — the harshest over-supply curve in
`MARKET_PARAMS` sitting on the shallowest book.
MILK is `linear, 1.60, T: 122`, and six cows never came near saturating it: milk held $143-$194 all
season with inventory pinned at I0.

`WOOL_SHOP_SETS_THE_HERD` is a **count** rule.
It reads "yarn store unlocked early, so cap cows and buy sheep" off Kawashigi's replays and never
asks how many wool units this town will drain.
That is the same shape of error `corner_the_book` was written to fix for carrot: a proxy standing in
for a quantity the observation reports directly.

This is also the milk lesson repeating on another product — see
`project_kaggriculture_milk_is_floored`, where cow COUNT was causal and `SHOP_ADAPT_HERD` read shops
rather than prices.

**Do not turn this into a fixed counterfactual.**
Selling those units is what moved the price, so "230 wool at $230 would have been $52,900" is not a
number the game could have produced.
The defensible statement is that the marginal sheep earned close to nothing.
