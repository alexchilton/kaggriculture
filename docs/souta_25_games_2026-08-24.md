## He is adaptive, and not kawashigi's shape

`scripts/is_he_a_tape.py` hashes every action he submits on each day and counts distinct hashes
across the 25 games. The start state is identical in every game
(`project_kaggriculture_start_state_is_fixed`), so a fixed script gives one hash per day.

```
 day  variants  largest block        day  variants  largest block
   0         1         25/25          15        19          3/25
   1         1         25/25          19        22          2/25
   2         2         15/25          21        25          1/25
   3         2         15/25          23        25          1/25
   5         4         12/25          26        25          1/25
   7         8         10/25          27        25          1/25
  11        14          4/25          28        20          2/25

first day that is not identical across all games: d2
first shop unlock: d3 in all 25
```

Two scripted days, then a clean two-way branch at **d2**, then continuous fan-out; by d21 no two of
25 games share a day.

The d2 branch is the interesting part. カワシギ's opening is fixed for 74 turns and ends exactly at
the d3 first shop unlock (`project_kaggriculture_leader_replans_on_shops`).
SOUTA branches a full day **before any shop is observable**, so d2 is conditioning on something
else — the opponent's visible play, or a d2 board fact.

**Caveat that limits this method:** a day can differ because he adapted, or because an earlier
purchase failed and left him a different bank. The d2 split at 15/25 is clean enough to look
deliberate; the later fan-out cannot be separated from drift this way.
