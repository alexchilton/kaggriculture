## What the arms actually did

`kagg/search/tape_edit.py:add_land` is the whole edit:

```python
out[step] = (farmer, hands, ([["BUY_LAND"]] + list(market))[:MAX_ORDERS])
```

One `BUY_LAND` order inserted at a chosen day and hour. Nothing else changes — no planting rows for
the new 25 tiles, no extra hires to work them, no seed bought for them.

And the new ground is unreachable by construction. On a replayed turn the unit actions are the
tape's verbatim (`schedule_agent.py:5789`), so every hand walks the route the q3 tape walked, on the
75 tiles the q3 tape used. No hand ever enters the fourth quadrant.

`LAND_PRICES = [1000, 2000, 4000]`, so the fourth parcel is **$4,000** spent on ground that is
provably never touched.

## There is no "real question" here. An addition is not available to us at all.

The obvious follow-up — *is a fourth parcel worth $4,000 if you actually farm it?* — cannot be asked
inside a replay, and an earlier draft of this document was wrong to lay out how to try.

Alex, 2026-08-22: *"i dont think an addition is actually possible due to hands and space so i think
that point is mute."*

Two independent reasons, either one sufficient:

- **The hands are the tape's.** `replayed()` sends the recorded `farmer` and `hands` verbatim on 719
  of 720 turns. A hand cannot be sent to a tile the tape never visited without abandoning the replay,
  and every route after that point desynchronises.
- **Hands and space are the binding constraint, not ground.** The crew the tape hires is sized for
  the tiles it farms. Twenty-five more tiles need more hands, which need more wage, on turns the
  tape spent elsewhere.

**So the general rule, and it is the useful thing to take from this:**

> Under a replay we own *quantities* and *labels* on turns the tape already chose. We do not own
> *additions*.

That is exactly the shape of everything that has worked — the `BUY_ANIMAL` species re-label, the
`BUY_SEED` count trim, the carrot `PLANT WHEAT -> PLANT CARROT` swap. Each keeps the turn, the tile
and the hand, and changes only what is being carried or sown.

`add_land` is the opposite shape. It adds a thing and creates no capacity to use it, which is why it
could only ever have lost. That was readable in five lines of `tape_edit.py` before the run.

## What is genuinely settled

**An unaccompanied `BUY_LAND` is a $4,000 loss, and there is no accompanied version available.**
Close the land question. Do not run another land arm.

The q3-versus-q4 gap that motivated the idea is real and is reached a different way: repointing
`(True, True)` at a four-quadrant *recording* was worth about +$20k a game. That works because the
recording farms its fourth quadrant — its own hands walk there, in its own turns. We get four
quadrants by choosing a tape that has them, never by buying one.

Which is the same conclusion as the tape shootout, arrived at from the other side: **the lever is
which tape we replay, not what we bolt onto it.**
