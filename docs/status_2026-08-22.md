## SETTLED

**Carrot has a narrow, real window: d25-d27.**
Sowing carrot outside it is a lifespan bug, not a crop-economics question.
The field sows wheat on a five-day cadence all season; the final bell truncates only the last three
sowings (d25, d26, d27), so only those tiles get less than wheat's full lifespan and only there does
carrot's shorter, faster yield (4 units at day 3) beat wheat's cut-off yield.
A wide sweep (d20-d25) lost **$6,787** on twelve tiles, all of it from extra weeds (control 25 weeds,
arm up to 32).
Narrowed to d25-d27, the weed count returns to 25 and the loss collapses to **$59** — measured on
seed 11, カワシギ opponent.

**Carrot pays when the town wants it and the opponent isn't already in it, but only then.**
`carrot_when_it_pays` — a runtime-conditional swap, decided once at d25 by reading the live town and
opponent board, not an offline static edit.
528 configurations, 48 towns:

```
town CA>=4, opp CA 1-14     n=125   mean +1,143   median  +503   75% improved
town CA>=4, mirror          n= 25   mean +1,659   median  +787   92% improved
town CA>=4, opp CA=0        n=100   mean   +273   median  -358   46% improved
town CA>=4, opp CA 15+      n= 25   mean   -510   median -1,219  28% improved
town CA<4, every bucket                    negative                0-11% improved
```

Unconditional, the same arm is **-$237** — the losing towns cancel the winning ones.
Both the town gate and the opponent gate matter; the town gate is the strong one, the opponent gate
mainly removes the CA-15+ bucket.

Drain-6 towns (the single best band, from the separate 3,648-season town grid) are about **11%** of
the true draw distribution, so this rule fires roughly one game in nine and is worth about **+$210
per game** in expectation.
That figure rests on 3 towns and 57 seasons — thin.

**Tomato is dead at every carrot-drain level.**
`-$675` to `-$7,040` across all seven drain bands, 2% win rate against the mirror.
No band is positive.
Do not chase it further without a new mechanism.

**No arm changes the win count in the eight-town head-to-head sweep.**
Control and all eleven carrot/tomato arms finish 4W-4L on the same four seeds.
Tomato is the clearest trap in the whole two days: **+$4,846 mean money** and **-$5,546 mean
margin** on the same arm, because it plants four fewer wheat tiles, sells less wheat into the book,
and the opponent's wheat book gets richer instead (seed 42: our inventory 9,532 vs control's 9,647;
the opponent's realized wheat margin against us goes from -$4,008 to -$20,692 with tomato in play).
This is the "select on win rate, not money" lesson landing in its most expensive form yet.

**Sell-hour timing is closed, five independent ways, all negative.**
Moving a day's whole sale to a fixed hour loses $4,698 (h23) to $26,601 (h0) — a single order can
only sell what the shed holds at that instant.
Adding an order on top, without removing the tape's own, is a wash (-$185 to -$236 at h8/h12/h16/
h20); done from d0 instead of d20 it is -$66,798.
Intraday WHEAT price range is under 2% (42.4 to 43.2), holds in the mirror.
Across six top players, median correlation between sell quantity and price is ~0, and only a third
of cells clear |r|>0.4 at n=8 — noise, not book-sizing.
The DAY and the PRODUCT matter (seasonal gradient WHEAT +81%, STRAWBERRY +58%, MILK +30%, WOOL
-98%); the HOUR does not.

**The field is a monoculture in opening, not in play style.**
1,010 deduplicated games plus the live leaderboard (5,724 teams).
38 of 58 sampled players emit カワシギ's identical first-72-turn action vector, spanning rank 7 to
rank 1288.
We are in that cluster too — we replay his tape — which is why every arm measured this week reads
within about ±$100 of control: against a copy of yourself, small edges are the only edges available.
Of the 18 agents in `data/opponent_pool_ranked.json`, roughly 12 are the same agent; effective
sample size is about 5, not 18.

**His one reactive rule is a single binary read, and it is opponent-driven, not shop-driven.**
26 of his games, 325 pairs.
Two seasons of his diverge at d3 if and only if the opponent's melon count crosses 9 — 100% of
pairs where that count differs diverge at d3, 0% where it doesn't, regardless of whether the two
seasons' shop draws matched.
The threshold is exactly `melon_branch.OPPONENT_MELON = 9`, already in our own code
(`kagg/search/melon_branch.py:77`), reverse-engineered before this measurement existed.

**Two coverage failures in the 3,648-season town grid were found and fixed.**
The q4 tape never armed — 0 of 76 combinations — because `q4_branch.DECIDE_BY_DAY = 5` needs a
YARN_STORE in the d3 slot and uniform town draws rarely put one there.
Fixed by `stratified_towns`, forcing a third of towns to a d3 yarn store.
Separately, the opponent pool sampled by leaderboard rank came out 7 cow-leaning to 2 sheep-leaning,
missing the three strongest sheep players entirely (StackKnight, Bruce, Eddy Despradel), and tomato
at zero for 8 of 10 sampled opponents.
Fixed by `BEHAVIOUR_SPAN` in `scripts/town_arm_grid.py`: cow 3-10, sheep 3-12, carrot 0-60, tomato
0-22.

## Corrections made to earlier claims

1. **Carrot window.** First claim: sweep d20-d25 broadly, wrong because it ignores lifespan —
   cost $6,787 for twelve tiles, all in extra weeds. Corrected: the real window is d25-d27 only,
   where the final bell truncates his five-day sowing cadence; loss drops to $59.
2. **Town shop-draw space.** First treated as enumerable (~70 draws). Corrected same day: the town
   draws 8 shops **with replacement** from 8 types, so the space is 8^8 ≈ 16.7 million — enumeration
   is not viable, sampling is required, and the eight towns used up to that point were drawn partly
   *because* four of them drain carrot, biasing the carrot arms upward.
3. **"He replans on the shop draw."** `is_the_field_open_loop.py` showed カワシギ diverging at d3 in
   65% of pairs and that was attributed to the shop unlock, the only d3 event anyone had thought of.
   Corrected: d3 carries two events (the first shop unlock AND the opponent's melon count crossing
   9), and separating them shows the shop draw contributes **0%** of the divergence — the melon
   threshold explains all of it.
4. **Market-timing correlation was a false zero.** An earlier pass averaged sell-quantity-vs-price
   correlation over every `(day, hour)` cell, 86% of which hold a constant zero, and reported
   `+0.00` for every player as if nobody times the market. Corrected: restricted to cells where
   quantity actually varies, median r is still ~0 for all six players measured, but that is now a
   real (if weak) reading, not an artifact of averaging in dead cells.
5. **The `(True, True)` replay key.** The comment justifying `(True, True)` sharing a file with
   `(True, False)` was correct when written — under `REPLAY_OPENING_DAYS` = d4-d11, the two tapes
   were provably indistinguishable, since they part company at d12, past the handover.
   `REPLAY_OPENING_DAYS` became all 30 days on 2026-08-21, the premise silently expired, and the key
   was not revisited until 2026-08-22. This is the "a decision to skip a fix can expire when the
   problem changes" failure mode, not a new bug class.
