Further changes, September 10, 2026:
actions.json holds 13 action tapes recovered from 109 public competition replays
of yhay81's own ladder submission, clustered by turn-level distance. They are
that author's current play, not the 13 tapes published in Shop Router 0909, and
they replace them one for one. The world -> tape map is that author's own,
recovered from which tape each shop pair was observed to run.
Three consequent edits to the inherited router, and nothing else:
  - the forced switch to tape index 2 at step 648 is removed. It is correct for
    the published set, where index 2 is a shared continuation. The recovered
    tapes are identical on turns 0..143 and all 13 diverge afterwards, endings
    included, so splicing one world's last 71 turns onto the others would submit
    sales against inventory those tapes never built.
  - the late expansion layer previews the replayed tape rather than index 2, to
    stay consistent with the above.
  - the terminal rescue no longer requires tape index 2; its own preconditions,
    which check the remaining tape for purchases, builds and planting, gate it.
No sale-window, repair, expansion or market logic is altered.
